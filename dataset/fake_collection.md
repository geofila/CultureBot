## Marble head of a youth (Μαρμάρινη κεφαλή νέου)
- URI: https://fake.example.org/item/0001

**Type:** Sculpture (Γλυπτό) / Archaeological object (Αρχαιολογικό αντικείμενο)
**Material:** Marble (Μάρμαρο)
**Time Period:** 101 AD - 200 AD [Roman Period (Ρωμαϊκή περίοδος)]
**Location Found:** Ancient Messene (Αρχαία Μεσσήνη)
**Dimensions:** Height: 24.0 cm, Width: 17.5 cm
**Inventory Number:** FME-0001

**Description:**
Life-size head of a young man carved in white Pentelic marble, broken at the neck. The
hair is rendered in deep drill channels and the pupils are incised, a treatment typical of
the second century AD. The nose is missing and the left cheek bears traces of burning,
probably from the destruction layer in which it was found. Πρόκειται για κεφαλή νέου από
λευκό πεντελικό μάρμαρο, εύρημα της ανασκαφής στην Αρχαία Μεσσήνη.

**Subjects & Themes:**
Antiquity (Αρχαιότητα), Portraiture (Προσωπογραφία), Sculpture (Γλυπτική)

## Bronze strigil (Χάλκινη ξύστρα)
- URI: https://fake.example.org/item/0002

**Type:** Metalwork (Μεταλλοτεχνία) / Athletic equipment (Αθλητικός εξοπλισμός)
**Material:** Bronze (Χαλκός)
**Time Period:** 400 BC - 300 BC [Classical Period (Κλασική περίοδος)]
**Location Found:** Olympia (Ολυμπία)
**Dimensions:** Length: 21.3 cm
**Inventory Number:** FME-0002

**Description:**
Curved bronze scraper used by athletes to remove oil and dust from the skin. The handle is
decorated with two incised parallel lines and ends in a flat loop for suspension. Found in
the filling of a Classical well together with fragments of black-glazed cups.

**Subjects & Themes:**
Athletics (Αθλητισμός), Daily life (Καθημερινή ζωή)

## Red-figure kylix (Ερυθρόμορφος κύλικας)
- URI: https://fake.example.org/item/0004

**Type:** Pottery (Κεραμική) / Drinking vessel (Αγγείο πόσης)
**Material:** Clay (Πηλός)
**Time Period:** 480 BC - 450 BC [Classical Period (Κλασική περίοδος)]
**Location Found:** Athens (Αθήνα)
**Dimensions:** Diameter: 28.5 cm, Height: 11.0 cm
**Inventory Number:** FME-0004

**Description:**
Shallow two-handled drinking cup. The interior medallion shows a symposiast reclining with
a lyre; the exterior carries a procession of five draped youths. Restored from eleven
fragments, with the foot largely modern. Attributed to the circle of the Brygos Painter.

**Subjects & Themes:**
Symposium (Συμπόσιο), Music (Μουσική), Vase painting (Αγγειογραφία)

**Time Period:** 1600 AD - 1650 AD [Ottoman Period (Οθωμανική περίοδος)]
**Location Found:** Ioannina (Ιωάννινα)
**Dimensions:** Height: 24.0 cm, Width: 24.0 cm, Thickness: 2.1 cm
**Inventory Number:** FME-0005

**Description:**

**Location Found:** Pella (Πέλλα)
**Dimensions:** Diameter: 1.8 cm, Weight: 8.6 g
**Inventory Number:** FME-0006
